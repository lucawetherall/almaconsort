**Help us keep the music playing and the next generation singing.**

We love performing for you, but the reality is that ticket sales alone are not able to secure the future of our choir, recording projects, exciting new concerts, and educational outreach. We rely on a community of supporters to bridge that gap.

Our **Friends Programme** is our foundational tier of support and is the easiest way to get closer to the choir.

As a friend you are directly supporting our **Alma Scholars** programme.

We are passionate about nurturing talent. Your contribution provides crucial mentoring, vocal coaching, and performance opportunities for young singers starting their careers. By becoming a Friend, you help these scholars find their voice and take their place on the stage alongside us.

---

## Saying Thank You

To show our appreciation for your support, we want to make sure you have the best experience possible every time you see us perform.

As a Friend, you'll receive:

- **The Best Seats in the House:** You get exclusive access to ticket sales **before the general public**. No rushing, no stress—just the seats you want.
- **A Little Appreciation Discount:** We offer Friends a **discount codes** on tickets for all our concerts. Bring a friend, or come to every show in the season!
- **Inside Access:** Missed a concert or just want to relive the moment? You'll get **exclusive early access** to concert recordings before we release them online.
