**Step inside the rehearsal room.**

The **Alma Partners** tier is for supporters who want to invest more substantially in the choir and get closer to the music-making.

A Partner's contribution expands what we can offer our **Alma Scholars**. Friends keep the programme running; Partners fund the specifics that turn a young singer into a professional: masterclasses, language coaching, and one-to-one mentoring.

---

## Partner Benefits

As a Partner you receive **all the benefits of a Friend** (priority booking, ticket discounts, and early access to recordings), plus:

- **Open rehearsals:** twice a year, sit in on a final rehearsal and watch the choir and scholars at work before performance night.
- **Your name in concert programmes**, unless you prefer to remain anonymous.
- **Early programmes:** receive the full concert programme before each performance.
- **Reserved seats** at every concert, on top of priority booking.
- **One free ticket a year:** a discount code for a complimentary ticket to bring a friend or family member.

## Join as an Alma Partner

**Monthly Contribution:** £20, £25, £30

Partners stand alongside us in our artistic work. Thank you for backing the choir and the next generation of singers.
